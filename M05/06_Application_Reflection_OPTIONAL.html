PEPROF 166 · MODULE 5

Sequence Learning Across a Year

Optional Application / Philosophy Connection

Purpose

Use one classroom example to explain a teaching decision and connect it to your developing understanding of the profession.

Your task

  1. Briefly describe the classroom situation in your own words.
  2. Identify a learning need or teaching responsibility.
  3. Explain one teacher action and why it could help.
  4. Add one sentence to your ongoing Teaching Philosophy document about what this example suggests you value as a future teacher.

Before you submit

Check the existing Canvas assessment directions and rubric. This draft is a suggested way to connect the module to your cumulative reflection, not an additional required assignment or a replacement for existing requirements.
