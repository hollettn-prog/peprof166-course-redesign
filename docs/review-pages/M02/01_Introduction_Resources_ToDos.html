Exploring the Teaching Profession

Introduction, Resources & To-Dos

Essential Question

What does it take to become a teacher in Wisconsin, and what responsibilities come with the job?

Why This Matters

You've probably seen teachers lead lessons, explain activities, and manage classrooms. But there's a lot more to teaching that students don't always see.

Teachers plan, work with families and colleagues, keep students safe, and make decisions throughout the school day. They also complete college coursework, field experiences, and licensing requirements before entering the profession.

This module gives you a closer look at those responsibilities. You'll also interview a practicing teacher to hear what the job is actually like.
