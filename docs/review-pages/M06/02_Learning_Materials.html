Start with a classroom moment

What a future teacher might notice

Changing equipment or space can improve access without abandoning the learning goal.

Think it through

What barrier could the teacher reduce?

Before reading the example response, write down what you would notice first.

An example of useful reasoning

Propose one change and explain what learning remains the same.

A strong response names a specific learner action, a teaching choice, and a reason. You do not need to use professional terminology perfectly yet.

Try it in a new situation

Imagine that you are observing this lesson as a visitor. What would you look or listen for to tell whether students are learning? Identify one observation and explain what it might tell the teacher.

Willie’s thinking prompt: What is the difference between students being busy and students learning?
