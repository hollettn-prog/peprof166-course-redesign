Philosophy Builder 1: The Purpose of HPE

Alternative existing assessment — verify before assigning

Choose one starting belief

Choose what matters most to you right now: Belonging (students can participate), Useful skills (learning beyond school), or Confidence (practice and improvement).

Write a short paragraph

Write about three to five sentences. Explain why your belief matters and connect it to one teacher action from the Learning Materials.

You can start with: “Right now, I connect most with…” “This matters to me because…” “One teacher action that supports this belief is…”

Save your work

Use the submission method specified in the existing Canvas assignment. Also save the paragraph in My Developing Teaching Philosophy so you can revisit it. Do not submit this as a second graded task unless the instructor has retained this assignment.
