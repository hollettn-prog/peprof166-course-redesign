Why this matters

A learning goal describes what students will demonstrate; evidence shows whether it happened.

Your module introduction

Watch your instructor’s captioned introduction in Canvas when available. The text on this page introduces the module while the recording is being prepared.

What you will practice

  • Explain one idea from the learning materials in your own words.
  • Use that idea to make a simple teaching decision.
  • Support your decision with something you noticed in a classroom example.

Resources and to-dos

  1. Learn: Read M4 Learning Materials and review any assigned Canvas resources.
  2. Quick check: Practice with the M4 scenario knowledge check if your instructor assigns it.
  3. Connect: Discuss your thinking using the existing Canvas discussion route, if assigned.
  4. Apply: Complete the existing graded assessment listed in Canvas; do not create a second submission.

Check the Canvas module for current deadlines, points, and submission instructions.
