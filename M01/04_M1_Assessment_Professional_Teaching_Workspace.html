PEPROF 166 · MODULE 1

Start Your Professional Teaching Workspace

Assessment — source-preserved 15-point draft

Why you are doing this

You will collect lesson ideas, observations, and resources during teacher preparation. Start a place to organize your work and a document where your ideas about teaching can grow.

Part 1 · Set up Google Drive

  1. Open Google Drive using your program-approved Google account.
  2. Create a folder named My Professional Teaching Resources.
  3. Inside it, create five folders: PEPROF 166 - Foundations; Teaching Philosophy; Lesson Plans and Teaching Ideas; Field Experiences and Observations; Professional Development and Certifications.

It is fine for most folders to be empty for now.

Part 2 · Start your philosophy document

  1. Open the Teaching Philosophy folder and create a Google Doc.
  2. Name it My Developing Teaching Philosophy.
  3. Add your name, PEPROF 166, and the heading Module 1: My Starting Beliefs About Teaching.

Part 3 · Write your first reflection

Write about 150–250 words addressing: What interests you about teaching? What should students gain from PE and health education, and how can adapted PE support students with disabilities? What is one quality you hope to bring to your future classroom?

Finish with: “I believe students deserve teachers who…” Your ideas can change as you learn.

Part 4 · Share and submit

  1. Share your main folder and Google Doc with your instructor using the email address provided in Canvas.
  2. Choose Viewer unless instructed otherwise. Do not make the entire folder publicly editable.
  3. In the existing Canvas Text Entry assignment, submit the link to My Professional Teaching Resources and the link to My Developing Teaching Philosophy.
  4. Confirm your instructor can open both links before submitting.

How it is evaluated

15 points in the exported assignment: folder organization 5; document naming 2; reflection covering three questions 5; teaching-belief sentence 1; working links and permissions 2. Verify the current Canvas assignment before replacing its rubric or points.
