Final ePortfolio: Evidence of the Educator You Are Becoming

Submit the working link to your completed ePortfolio. Your portfolio should tell a coherent story of professional growth, not function as a folder of unrelated assignments.

Include

  • A brief statement of your emerging philosophy of health, physical, and adapted physical education
  • Selected evidence from across the course, with context for each item
  • An explanation of the instructional decision each item demonstrates
