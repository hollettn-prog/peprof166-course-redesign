PE Teacher Interview: From Experience to Professional Action

Purpose: Learn how an educator makes real professional decisions, then use that evidence to identify your next step.

Complete the interview

Use the Physical Education Teacher Interview document. Ask follow-up questions that invite a concrete example: “What did you do?” “What made that difficult?” “What happened next?”

Submit

  1. A concise record of the educator's responses.
  2. Three evidence-based findings: a professional responsibility, a decision or tradeoff, and a surprise or misconception corrected.
  3. One connection: explain how a response connects to Chapter 3.
  4. One action: name a specific step you will take before clinical practice, when you will take it, and what evidence will show progress.

Success criteria

  • You synthesize rather than only transcribe.
  • Your claims are supported by interview evidence.
  • Your action step is specific and feasible.
  • You protect the educator's privacy and do not include information unrelated to the assignment.
