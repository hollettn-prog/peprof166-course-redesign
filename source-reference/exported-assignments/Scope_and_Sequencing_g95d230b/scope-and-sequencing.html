Scope and Sequence: Build a Defensible Year Plan

Design challenge: Create a progression of learning, not only a calendar of activities.

Review the module resources, then use the Scope and Sequence Assignment.

Your plan should make visible

  • The learners and context
  • Priority standards and outcomes
  • How learning becomes more complex over time
  • Where learners practice and demonstrate growth
  • How time, equipment, facilities, seasons, and access affect decisions

Decision memo

Along with the completed plan, explain three major choices. For each, name the constraint or learner need it addresses, the benefit, and one tradeoff. Identify one revision you made after checking coverage, progression, or feasibility.

Success criteria

  • The sequence develops learning rather than repeating activities.
  • Standards and evidence are visible.
  • The plan is feasible in the stated context.
  • Access and meaningful participation are considered throughout.
