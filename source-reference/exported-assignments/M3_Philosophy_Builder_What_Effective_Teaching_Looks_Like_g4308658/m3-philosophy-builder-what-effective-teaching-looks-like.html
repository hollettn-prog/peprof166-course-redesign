Philosophy Builder: What Effective Teaching Looks Like

Identify the teaching practices that make learning, participation, and growth visible.

Purpose

Effective teaching involves more than keeping students active, organized, or engaged. Physical education teachers make intentional decisions that help students develop skills, build confidence, and demonstrate meaningful learning.

In this Philosophy Builder reflection, you'll move beyond general statements about "good teaching" to identify specific, observable practices. Your ideas will help you describe what effective instruction looks like in your final teaching philosophy.

YOUR PHILOSOPHY BUILDER REFLECTION

What Effective Teaching Looks Like: Describe the teacher actions, student behaviors, and evidence that demonstrate effective physical education instruction.

Your Task

Write a 150–250-word reflection addressing the following questions:

Reflection Questions

  1. Recognizing effective teaching: What would you expect to see and hear in an effectively taught physical education lesson?
  2. Teacher influence: Which teacher action has the greatest influence on student learning or participation?
  3. Evidence of learning: What evidence would tell you that students are learning rather than simply staying busy?

Complete Your Reflection

Conclude your reflection with one sentence beginning:

Effective physical education teaching is visible when…

Your concluding statement should capture your developing belief about what makes physical education instruction effective.

What to Include

Before submitting, review your reflection to make sure it includes:

  • Observable teaching practices: Describe specific teacher and student actions that demonstrate effective instruction.
  • Purposeful teacher action: Identify a teaching practice that you believe has a meaningful influence on student learning or participation.
  • Evidence of learning: Explain how you would recognize learning rather than simply student activity or compliance.
  • Connection to your teaching beliefs: Explain why these practices matter to the kind of physical education teacher you want to become.
  • Concluding statement: End with the required sentence starter and stay within approximately 150–250 words.

Create and Submit

Write your reflection directly in the Canvas Text Entry submission area.

You do not need to upload a separate document. Your response should be thoughtful, specific, and connected to the ideas explored in this module.
