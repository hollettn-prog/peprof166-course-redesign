Appropriate-Practice Audit and Improvement Plan

Purpose: Use SHAPE America's guidance to analyze practice and prioritize a realistic improvement.

Read the provided Appropriate Practices in School-Based Health Education document.

Part 1: Build a usable framework

For each of the six practice areas, write a one-sentence “look-for”: what would students or an observer see when the practice is implemented well?

Part 2: Audit a realistic program

Use the module's program snapshot or a course-approved example. Identify two strengths and two risks. Support every claim with an observable detail and a connection to the guidance.

Part 3: Prioritize improvement

Select one high-leverage improvement. Explain the action, intended effect on learners, resources or support required, evidence of success, and likely implementation challenge.

Success criteria

  • You apply the guidance rather than only summarize it.
  • The improvement protects privacy and supports inclusion.
  • The recommendation is feasible and produces visible evidence.
  • Your professional-growth need is tied to an action or resource.
