Assessment Scenario: What Does the Evidence Tell You?

Purpose: Select an assessment that matches the learning target, interpret the resulting evidence, and decide what to do next.

Complete the linked scenario in Canvas

  1. Name the learning target in observable terms.
  2. Select or design an assessment that captures that target.
  3. Explain why the assessment fits better than at least one alternative.
  4. Interpret the student evidence: what appears learned, developing, or unclear?
  5. Choose the next instructional response for the class and for learners needing additional support or challenge.

Submit

Download and upload your completed answer page.

Success criteria

  • Target, task, and evidence are aligned.
  • Your interpretation refers to evidence rather than assumptions.
  • Your next step responds to the evidence.
  • The assessment offers a fair way for learners to demonstrate the intended learning.
