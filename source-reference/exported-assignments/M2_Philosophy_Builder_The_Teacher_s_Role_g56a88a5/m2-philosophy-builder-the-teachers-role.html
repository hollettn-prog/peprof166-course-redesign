The Teacher’s Role

Clarify the responsibilities and professional commitments that should guide your work as an educator.

Purpose

This reflection asks you to consider what effective physical education teachers do beyond planning activities. Your response will help define the teacher’s role in your final teaching philosophy.

Instructions

Write a 150–250-word reflection addressing the following questions:

  1. What did you learn about the realities and responsibilities of being a physical education teacher?

  2. Which professional quality or responsibility matters most to you, and why?

  3. How should a teacher’s daily decisions communicate care, competence, and respect for learners?

Conclude with one sentence beginning:

As a physical educator, I am responsible for...

Assignment Expectations

A complete reflection identifies an important professional responsibility, explains why it matters, and connects it to a specific example from the module or teacher interview.

Before submitting, confirm that your response:

  • Addresses all three questions.

  • Uses at least one specific example.

  • Explains how professional decisions affect students.

  • Ends with the required statement.

  • Is approximately 150–250 words.

Evaluation and Submission

Submit your reflection as a text-entry assignment in Canvas.

This assignment is worth 5 points and will be evaluated for thoughtful completion. Full credit is earned when the response addresses the prompt, makes a clear connection to module learning, and includes the required professional commitment.
