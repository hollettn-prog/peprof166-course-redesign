Aligned Lesson Segment: Objective and Instant Activity

Design challenge: Create an instant activity that prepares learners for the objective rather than merely keeping them busy.

Plan first

  • Learners/grade level and context
  • Standard or learning priority
  • What learners should be able to do
  • What evidence would show progress
  • Likely access or participation barrier

Complete and submit

Use the Lesson Objective and Instant Activity document. Include your measurable objective, activity directions, equipment/space, a transition plan, one access support, and a brief alignment explanation.

Before submitting

  • The objective describes observable learning.
  • The activity gives every learner meaningful practice.
  • Directions and transitions are feasible and safe.
  • The access support preserves dignity and an appropriate level of challenge.
  • You can explain how the activity prepares learners for the objective.
