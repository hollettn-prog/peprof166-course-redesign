PE Observation Mission: Evidence to Instructional Decision

Purpose: Practice noticing teaching with enough precision to make a useful recommendation.

Before the observation

Select one lens: access and participation, clarity and transitions, feedback, motivation, or behavior support. Review the observation form and write three observable “look-fors.”

During the observation

  • Record what is said or done before interpreting it.
  • Capture at least three pieces of evidence related to your lens.
  • Notice variation: who participates, waits, receives feedback, or encounters a barrier?

Submit

  1. The completed observation checklist.
  2. One evidence-rich moment: context, teacher action, learner response.
  3. Your interpretation using a concept from Chapters 4–6.
  4. One recommended adjustment, its intended effect, and the evidence you would collect next.
Professional expectation: Remove student names and identifying details. Describe behavior and learning evidence without diagnosing motives.
