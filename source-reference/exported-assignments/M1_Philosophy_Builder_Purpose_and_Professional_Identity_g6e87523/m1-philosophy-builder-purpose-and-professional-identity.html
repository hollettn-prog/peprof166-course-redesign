Philosophy Builder 1: The Purpose of HPE

Assignment

Begin a short record of what you believe matters in teaching. You’ll return to this entry as your ideas develop.

Choose a starting belief

Choose one statement that fits your thinking right now. Several may appeal to you; select one to begin.

  • Belonging: I want students to feel that they belong and can participate.
  • Useful skills: I want students to develop skills they can use beyond school.
  • Confidence: I want students to build confidence through practice and improvement.

There is no single correct belief. I’m looking for a clear connection between your choice, your reason, and an example.

Build from your discussion

Look back at what you hoped students would gain from your teaching. You can use that same idea here.

Write a short paragraph using these sentence starters:

  1. “Right now, I connect most with the idea that…”
  2. “This matters to me because…”
  3. “One teacher action that supports this belief is…”

Connect your last sentence to an example from the Learning Materials. About three to five sentences is enough. You do not need outside research or a complete teaching philosophy.

An example

“Right now, I connect most with helping students build confidence through practice. This matters to me because students need chances to try again as they learn. In the target activity, the teacher gives one useful tip before the next attempt. That gives the student something specific to try.”

This shows how to connect a belief to an example. Use the belief and reason that fit your own thinking.

Before you submit

  • I named a starting belief.
  • I explained why it matters to me.
  • I connected it to a teacher action from the Learning Materials.

Save and submit

Enter your paragraph in the Canvas assignment text box. Save the same entry in your developing teaching philosophy document so you can revisit it later. You do not need to upload a separate file.

Use my feedback to clarify your thinking. Your belief can change as you encounter more teaching examples.
