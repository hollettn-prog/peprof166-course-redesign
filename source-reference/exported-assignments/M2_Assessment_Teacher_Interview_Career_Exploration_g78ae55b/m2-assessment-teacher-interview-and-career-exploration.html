M2 Assessment: Teacher Interview & Career Exploration

Learning Outcome

Describe professional teaching responsibilities, identify the basic Wisconsin licensure pathway, and use a teacher interview to explain what you are learning about the profession.

Big Question

What can I learn from someone who is already doing the job I am preparing to do?

Your Assignment

For this assignment, you'll interview a practicing teacher to learn about their work, responsibilities, and experiences.

You will also review the basic steps toward becoming licensed in Wisconsin and add a short reflection to your developing teaching philosophy.

You do not need to know how to teach yet. The goal is to learn more about the profession from someone with experience.

Part 1: Find a Teacher to Interview

Interview a current or recently practicing physical education, health education, or adapted physical education teacher.

Your interview may take place in person, by phone, or through a video call. Allow approximately 15–20 minutes.

Contact the teacher ahead of time, explain that you're completing an introductory college assignment, and arrange a time that works for them.

Need Help Finding Someone?

You could contact a former teacher or a local school. If you cannot arrange an interview, contact me before the due date so we can discuss an alternative.

Part 2: Ask Your Interview Questions

Use the questions below to guide your conversation. You may ask follow-up questions if something interests you.

Teacher Interview Questions

  1. Getting started: What made you decide to become a teacher?
  2. Daily work: What are some important responsibilities you have besides teaching lessons?
  3. Preparing for the profession: What do you remember about becoming a licensed teacher?
  4. Rewards: What do you enjoy most about teaching?
  5. Challenges: What is one challenge that new teachers should be prepared for?
  6. Advice: What advice would you give someone beginning a teacher preparation program?

Take notes during the conversation. You do not need to record the interview, and you should not include private information about the teacher's students.

Part 3: Explore Your Wisconsin Licensure Pathway

Visit the Wisconsin DPI Educator Licensing website and review the information in this module.

In your assignment document, answer these two questions:

  1. What are three general steps involved in preparing to become a licensed teacher in Wisconsin?
  2. Where can you find official information or ask questions about your specific licensing requirements?

You do not need to memorize license codes or list every requirement. Focus on understanding the pathway and where to find accurate information.

Part 4: What Did You Learn?

Write a short reflection of approximately 150–200 words addressing:

  1. What surprised you or stood out during the interview?
  2. What did you learn about the responsibilities of being a teacher?
  3. How did the interview influence what you think about becoming a teacher?

Use at least one specific example or comment from your interview to support your reflection.
