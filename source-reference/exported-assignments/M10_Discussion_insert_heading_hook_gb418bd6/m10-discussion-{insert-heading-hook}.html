EXAMPLE

 

Discussion

Apply your knowledge

In this discussion, you'll practice articulating the value of elementary PE to non-specialist audiences and using evidence and school context to evaluate a program's strengths, weaknesses, and opportunities for improvement.

Maple Grove Elementary

Open House Preparation

Ms. Jordan, the first-year PE teacher, has just received an email from Principal Martinez ahead of Open House.

📝 Note: You can revisit the Introduction to Maple Grove Elementary at any time for details on the school, teacher, and students.

Maple Grove Elementary School

Home of the Mustangs

From: Principal Martinez <martinez@maplegrove.k12.wi.us>

To: Ms. Jordan <jordan@maplegrove.k12.wi.us>

Subject: Welcome to Maple Grove – A Few Notes Before Open House

Hi Ms. Jordan,

Welcome aboard! We're thrilled to have you join our team this year.

A quick heads-up: at Open House next week, I expect a few parents to ask why physical education matters when students already get recess. I've attached our school profile so you have a good sense of who we serve here at Maple Grove – it might help as you think through your response.

Could you prepare a short, parent-friendly explanation of why PE matters for our students?

Thanks so much, and again, welcome to the team!

Best,
Principal Martinez

 

Your task

1. Initial Post

Considering the pending Maple Grove Open House and the school/program profile that Principal Martinez shared with Ms. Jordan, respond to the following:

  1. What should Ms. Jordan tell parents about the purpose and value of elementary PE, and how is it different from recess? Support your answer with course concepts and/or outside evidence (e.g., SHAPE America standards).
  2. Using the Maple Grove Elementary School Profile linked in Principal Martinez's email above, identify strengths and weaknesses of the current PE program's ability to meet the needs of this school community.
  3. Based on the strengths and weaknesses you identified, what is one recommendation you would offer Ms. Jordan for improving the program? Your recommendation should be realistic given Maple Grove's budget, facilities, and instructional time.

Make your initial post by the due date in the course calendar.

2. Reply Posts

Respond to at least two classmates by:

  • Identifying one idea from their post you would add to your own response to parents.
  • Explaining why that idea would resonate with Maple Grove families specifically.

Post your replies by the due date in the course calendar.

Evaluation

Note: You must post to the discussion board before you will be able to see what other students have posted. This is meant to encourage original thinking and give you the opportunity to objectively compare your thoughts with others.

Your answers will be evaluated using the criteria in the attached rubric. To view this rubric, click on the three dots in the upper right-hand side of the screen and select Show Rubric.
