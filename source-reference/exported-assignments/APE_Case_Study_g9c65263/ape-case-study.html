APE Case Study: Remove Barriers, Preserve the Goal

Start with the learner, not the label. The cases provide limited information. Identify what you still need to know instead of assuming that a diagnosis determines needs or supports.

For each of the three cases

  1. State the intended learning goal.
  2. Identify learner strengths and at least two missing pieces of information.
  3. Identify a likely barrier in the task, environment, equipment, rules, or communication.
  4. Propose three accommodations and three modifications. Label each accurately and explain how it affects the learning goal.
  5. Select the two strongest supports and justify them using safety, access, agency, challenge, and feasibility.
  6. Describe the evidence you would collect to learn whether each support worked.
  7. Explain who you would consult and how learner voice would inform the next adjustment.

Success criteria

  • Supports respond to an identified barrier.
  • Rationales explain why a support fits; lists alone are insufficient.
  • Recommendations maintain dignity and meaningful participation.
  • You avoid promises or conclusions that require information not provided.
