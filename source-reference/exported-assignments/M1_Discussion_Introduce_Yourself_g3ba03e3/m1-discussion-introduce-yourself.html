Introduce Yourself

Discussion

Your post

I’d like to hear what interests you as you begin this course. Choose the statement that best fits you right now:

  • I’m curious about teaching physical education.
  • I’m curious about teaching health education.
  • I’m curious about adapted physical education.
  • I’m interested in more than one area or still deciding.

Introduce yourself using the name you’d like classmates to use. In a few sentences, explain your choice and name one thing you hope students would gain from your teaching.

Sentence starters:

  • “Right now, I’m curious about… because…”
  • “One thing I hope students would gain is…”

You may refer to an example from the Learning Materials. You do not need to share private experiences or describe a personal health concern.

Reply to one classmate

Make one specific connection to their post or ask a question about their teaching interest. A brief reply is enough.

For example: “You mentioned helping students feel comfortable participating. I connect with that because having a chance to practice helped me feel more confident.”

Use the same work

You can reuse an idea from your post in Philosophy Builder 1. You don’t need to develop a new topic for that entry.
