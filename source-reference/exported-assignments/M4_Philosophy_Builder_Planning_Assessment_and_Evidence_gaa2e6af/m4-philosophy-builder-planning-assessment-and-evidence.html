Philosophy Builder: Planning, Assessment, and Evidence

Connecting what you teach, how students practice, and how you recognize learning.

Purpose

Effective physical education teaching involves more than planning activities and assigning grades. Teachers make intentional decisions about what students should learn, how they will practice, and what evidence will demonstrate their progress.

In this Philosophy Builder reflection, you'll consider how planning and assessment work together to support meaningful learning. Your ideas will help shape the way you describe your instructional decisions in your final teaching philosophy.

YOUR PHILOSOPHY BUILDER REFLECTION

Planning, Assessment, and Evidence: Explain how you will connect learning objectives, student practice, and assessment to make informed teaching decisions.

Your Task

Write a 150–250-word reflection addressing the following questions:

Reflection Questions

  1. Connecting instruction: How should learning objectives, practice opportunities, and assessment work together in physical education?
  2. Purpose of assessment: What should assessment do for learners and teachers beyond producing a grade?
  3. Responding to evidence: How will you respond when evidence shows that students did not learn what you intended?

Complete Your Reflection

Conclude your reflection with one sentence beginning:

I will know meaningful learning is occurring when…

Your concluding statement should communicate what you believe meaningful learning looks like and how you will recognize it as a teacher.

What to Include

Before submitting, review your reflection to make sure it includes:

  • Instructional alignment: Explain how objectives, practice, and assessment should support one another.
  • Meaningful assessment: Describe how assessment helps students understand their progress and teachers make decisions.
  • Responsive teaching: Identify what you would do when students are not demonstrating the intended learning.
  • Personal teaching beliefs: Connect your response to the kind of physical education teacher you want to become.
  • Concluding statement: End with the required sentence starter and stay within approximately 150–250 words.

Create and Submit

Write your reflection directly in the Canvas Text Entry submission area.

You do not need to upload a separate document. Your response should be thoughtful, specific, and connected to the ideas explored in this module.
