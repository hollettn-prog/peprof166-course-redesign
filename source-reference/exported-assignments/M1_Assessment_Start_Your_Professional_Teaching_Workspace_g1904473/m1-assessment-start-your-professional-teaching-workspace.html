M1 Assessment: Start Your Professional Teaching Workspace

Learning Outcome

Create an organized Google Drive workspace and begin a teaching philosophy document that explains your initial beliefs about physical education, health education, and adapted physical education.

Big Question

What kind of teacher do I want to become, and how can I start preparing now?

Getting Started

Throughout your teacher preparation program, you'll create lesson plans, complete observations, collect teaching resources, and develop your own ideas about teaching.

You'll need a place to keep that work organized. You'll also need a way to look back at how your thinking changes as you learn more about the profession.

In this assignment, you'll set up your professional Google Drive folders and create a Google Doc for your developing teaching philosophy. You'll continue using both throughout your program.

Part 1: Set Up Your Google Drive

Open Google Drive using the Google account approved for your teacher preparation program.

  1. Click New and select New folder.
  2. Name the folder My Professional Teaching Resources.
  3. Open the folder and create the five subfolders shown below.

Your Folder Organization

Your Google Drive should look like this:

My Professional Teaching Resources

  • PEPROF 166 - Foundations
  • Teaching Philosophy
  • Lesson Plans and Teaching Ideas
  • Field Experiences and Observations
  • Professional Development and Certifications

Most of these folders will be empty for now. That's fine. You'll add work to them in this course and in future classes.

Part 2: Create Your Teaching Philosophy Google Doc

A teaching philosophy explains what you believe about teaching and learning. You don't need a finished philosophy yet. You'll develop one as you move through the program.

  1. Open your Teaching Philosophy folder.
  2. Click New and choose Google Docs.
  3. Name your document My Developing Teaching Philosophy.
  4. At the top of the document, type your name and PEPROF 166.
  5. Add the heading Module 1: My Starting Beliefs About Teaching.

Part 3: Write Your First Reflection

Under your Module 1 heading, write approximately 150–250 words answering the following questions.

Reflection Questions

  1. Why teaching? What interests you about becoming a teacher?
  2. What matters? What should students gain from physical education and health education? How can adapted PE help students with disabilities learn?
  3. Looking ahead: What is one quality you hope to bring to your future classroom?

Finish your reflection with one sentence beginning:

I believe students deserve teachers who...

Write what you currently believe. You can use an experience from your own schooling or an example from this module to explain your thinking.

You'll return to this same document throughout the semester, so don't worry about making your first reflection perfect.

Part 4: Share Your Work

You'll submit links to your Google Drive folder and Teaching Philosophy Google Doc.

How to Share Your Files

  1. Open the file or folder you want to share.
  2. Click Share.
  3. Add your instructor's email address using the sharing instructions provided in Canvas.
  4. Choose Viewer access unless instructed otherwise.
  5. Copy the link.
  6. Repeat these steps for your Teaching Philosophy Google Doc.

A Note About Sharing

Keep your professional resources private unless you need to share them for a course assignment. Your instructor should be able to view your work, but you should not make your entire folder publicly editable.

What to Submit

In the Canvas Text Entry submission box, paste these two links:

  1. My Professional Teaching Resources: Link to your main Google Drive folder.
  2. My Developing Teaching Philosophy: Link to your Google Doc containing your first reflection.

You do not need to upload a PDF or separate document.

Evaluation

This assignment is worth 15 points.
