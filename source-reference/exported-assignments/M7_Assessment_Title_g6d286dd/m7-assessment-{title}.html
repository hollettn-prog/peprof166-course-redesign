M1 Toolkit Project: My Philosophy of Elementary PE

How do YOU feel about Elementary PE?

Purpose

Throughout this course, you will build an Elementary Physical Education Teacher Toolkit by creating practical resources that support your future teaching. Each module adds a new artifact to your toolkit, allowing you to apply course concepts while developing materials you can use during field experiences, student teaching, and beyond.

In this module, you explored the purpose and value of quality elementary PE, developmentally appropriate practice, and the role of physical education in supporting the whole child. The purpose of this assignment is to develop a personal philosophy that reflects your beliefs about teaching and learning in elementary PE. This philosophy will serve as the foundation of your professional toolkit.

Instructions

  1. Open the M1 Who I Am as a PE Teacher folder in your Teaching Toolkit folder. 📝Note: To review the teaching toolkit project and the instructions for downloading and sharing the toolkit templates, see Teaching Toolkit Project: Introduction & Sneak Peek.
  2. Complete the Elementary PE Philosophy template by:
    • Explaining your beliefs about the purpose of elementary physical education
    • Identifying three core beliefs that will guide your teaching
    • Writing a vision statement describing the experiences you want every child to have in your future PE program
    • Connecting your philosophy to ideas explored in this module
  3. Save your completed artifact in the Module 1 folder.
  4. Submit the Google Drive link to your professional toolkit.

🛑 Important: Make sure your Google toolkit folder has been shared with your instructor.

Assignment Expecations

Unless otherwise noted, all toolkit artifacts should:

  • Be completed using the provided template
  • Demonstrate thoughtful application of the module concepts
  • Be organized, professional, and ready for future classroom use
  • Use clear writing and appropriate grammar, spelling, and professional language
  • Include sufficient detail to demonstrate your understanding of the course content

Evaluation and Submission

Rather than uploading individual files, you will submit a Google Drive link to your Elementary PE Teacher Toolkit.

Before submitting, make sure:

  • Your completed artifact is saved in the correct module folder
  • Your Google Drive sharing settings allow your instructor to view the folder
  • Your toolkit includes all previously completed artifacts

Refer to the grading rubric for further details. For tips on how to locate the feedback on your assessment, visit reviewing comments and viewing annotations.

Tip: Use the rubric to evaluate your work before submitting it.

Please submit your work by 11:59 pm (CST) on the date listed in the course calendar.

Submit the assignment to this page by clicking Start Assignment and uploading the URL to your Google Drive in the Website URL area that appears.
