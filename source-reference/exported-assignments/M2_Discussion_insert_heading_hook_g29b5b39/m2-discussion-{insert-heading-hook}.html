M2 Discuss: What Makes a Good Teacher?

Learning Outcome

Identify an important professional responsibility and explain how it affects students' learning or school experiences.

Why We're Discussing This

Teachers have many responsibilities, and some are easier to notice than others. This discussion asks you to think about what matters most in a teacher's daily work.

The Teaching Situation

Two new teachers are preparing for their first year.

Teacher A spends time creating fun activities and wants students to enjoy every lesson.

Teacher B focuses on setting clear expectations, learning about students' needs, and checking whether students are improving.

Your question: What could each teacher learn from the other?

Part 1: Share Your Thinking

Write approximately 100–150 words addressing these questions:

  1. COMPARE: What is one strength of each teacher's approach?
  2. CHOOSE: Which professional responsibility do you think is especially important for a beginning teacher?
  3. EXPLAIN: Why does that responsibility matter for students?

Part 2: Learn From a Classmate

Read your classmates' responses and reply to one classmate.

Identify one point you agree with or see differently. Explain your thinking and, if helpful, offer another example.

Your response should add something to the discussion rather than simply saying "I agree."

Think About Your Own Teaching

As you prepare for your teacher interview, consider whether the teacher you interview identifies the same responsibilities you discussed here. You do not need to submit another response.

Discussion Expectations

  • Use examples from the teaching situation or module materials.
  • Explain your thinking in your own words.
  • Respectfully consider a different perspective.
  • Do not include identifiable information about students or classrooms.

What to Complete

  • Initial response: Approximately 100–150 words.
  • Peer response: One thoughtful reply.

Evaluation

This discussion is worth 5 points.

Criteria Points
Identifies strengths of both approaches 1
Identifies and explains an important teacher responsibility 2
Completes one meaningful peer response 2
Total 5
